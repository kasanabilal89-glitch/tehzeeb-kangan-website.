About Tehzeeb Kangan

“Tehzeeb” means courtesy, culture and refinement, and that is how we want every guest to feel when they sit down with us in Kangan.

We cook Kashmiri favourites and everyday dishes with fresh ingredients, balanced spices and careful preparation, so each plate tastes the way good food should.

Whether you are stopping by for a quick snack, a family meal or a cup of noon chai, you are welcome here.

Quality foodIngredients chosen with care and dishes prepared to a standard we are happy to serve.
FreshnessMeals made with fresh ingredients, from rich gravies to quick bites.
Real tasteTraditional Kashmiri flavours that stay true to the dish.
